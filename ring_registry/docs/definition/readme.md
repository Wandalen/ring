### The Shape Every Definition Found

Stated once because most of the thirteen definitions reach it independently:
**this crate is the family's outlier by construction — the only one of thirty-three
holding a `HashMap`, one of two writing a reasoned `allow`, the only one returning
an error with the caller's payload beside it — and it argues each unusual choice at
unusual length, from the source, in prose nothing measures; measured, the arguments
come apart in three different directions.** Two hundred and twenty-three lines hold
eight methods, twenty of those lines justify one lint suppression, and the one
construct on the same path that costs measurable time is unremarked.
