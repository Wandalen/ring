### Five Reachable, Fifty-Eight Recorded

Most of the table is `n/a`, and that is the headline rather than an accident of
scoring. `ring_gating` computes almost nothing — no ordering, no fold, no
arithmetic, no loop, no `unsafe`, no destructor — so there is very little in it
that *can* be wrong. Its correctness is inherited from `ring_cursor` and
`ring_seqno`, enforced by manual source readings, and confirmed by a suite that
exercises exactly one lap against exactly one stall.

What the recorded findings are about instead is **cost and reach**: a `Vec` per
gating read that nothing measures (GT38, GT39), a plural shape nothing
constructs (GT13, GT58, GT60), a third rung nobody calls (GT17, GT62), a width
that disagrees with its sibling (GT51), and a concurrency test that cannot fail
(GT40).

The five that *are* reachable share a shape: each is a place where the crate's
safety rests on something outside the compiler's reach.
